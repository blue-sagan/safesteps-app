Privacy Policy

SafeSteps is a road-safety app for Kenya, built with the Kenya Scouts Association as Project 360° Shield. It is operated by [operator legal name] ("we", "us"). This page explains what the app stores, where it is stored, and what you can do about it.

The short version: most of what you enter never leaves your phone. SafeSteps works with no account and no connection. Only data you explicitly save to an account is sent to a server, and this page lists exactly what that is.

1. Data that stays on your device

The app keeps your progress in your browser's local storage under the key safesteps_state_v3. It is not uploaded anywhere unless you sign in and use a feature that saves to your account.

2. Data stored on the server, if you create an account

Creating an account is optional. If you do, the following is stored in our database (hosted on Supabase) and is visible only to you:

Passwords are never stored by us in readable form. They are handled by Supabase Auth, which stores a one-way hash. Nobody at SafeSteps can read your password, and we will never ask you for it. Passwords must be 6 to 8 characters.

Row-level security is enabled on every table. Access is enforced by the database itself, not just by application code: a request can only read or write rows that belong to the signed-in user. Reports you submit are visible to you; the community hazard summary is built from aggregate counts only.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks. There is no advertising SDK in the app.
  • No third-party analytics, no tracking pixels, and no advertising SDK. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, and no vendor script of any kind.
  • No selling or renting of personal data. Ever.
  • No location tracking in the background. Location is used only when you are on a feature that needs it and you allow it — for a hazard report, route planning or SOS.
  • No access to your contacts. A trusted contact is typed in by you.

3a. Anonymous usage counts (our own, first-party)

So that we can tell whether the app is working and which parts people actually use, SafeSteps records a small number of event counts on its own server. This is not a third-party analytics service: nothing is loaded from a vendor, no cookie is set, and no profile is built.

What is recordedWhat is never recorded
An event name — e.g. app opened, a screen was viewed (by name), a sign-in succeeded or failed, a report was submitted — plus a short coded value such as the screen name, an HTTP status, or a failure category. Your identity in any form: no account id, no email, no IP address stored with the event, no device or browser fingerprint, no session id, no free text, no location, and nothing you typed.

The database table behind this has no column that could hold an identifier, so events cannot be traced back to a person even by us. Counts are deleted automatically after 180 days.

You can turn it off. Settings → Anonymous app telemetry. When it is off, nothing is sent at all. The switch is honoured immediately and remembered on your device. The app works identically either way.

Error reports are counts too: we record that an error of a given type happened (network, JavaScript, storage, permission) — never the error message, the stack trace or any value in it, because those routinely contain what a user typed.

5. How long we keep things

  • Account data: until you delete it (see §6).
  • Security events: automatically deleted after 365 days.
  • Email delivery records: automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Verification codes and rate-limit counters: deleted a day after they expire.
  • On your device: until you clear the app's site data or uninstall it.

6. Your control over your data

Delete your account and everything on our server: open the app → Account → Delete My Account & Server Data. This erases your account, your ability to sign in, and everything the server holds for you: profile, reports, quiz results, family profile, vault entries and security log. The same email and password will no longer work afterwards. It is not a request that someone processes later — it happens when you tap it, and it cannot be undone.

Delete everything on your device: clear the app's site data in your browser settings (or uninstall the app). That removes your saved progress, medical note and trusted contact from the phone.

Access or correct your data: most of it is on the Account and Settings screens. For anything else, contact [contact email].

If you are in Kenya, the Data Protection Act 2019 gives you the rights to be informed, to access, to correct, to delete and to object to processing. If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR gives you equivalent rights. We honour these requests regardless of where you are. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya) or your local supervisory authority.

7. Children

SafeSteps is used in schools and by Scouts, so many users are under 18. The app is designed so that a child can use it without an account — nothing needs to be uploaded for the lessons, quizzes, checklists or route planner to work.

  • We do not knowingly collect data from a child under 18 without the involvement of a parent, guardian, teacher or Scout leader at the point an account is created.
  • We do not profile children, show them advertising, or sell their data.
  • Where a school or Scout group sets up accounts for minors, that organisation is responsible for obtaining consent from parents or guardians.
  • A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's account at [contact email].

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are hashed by the authentication provider. Server-side secrets are never sent to the browser. The database enforces per-user access at row level. Sign-in attempts are rate limited and logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please report it to [contact email] rather than publishing it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the "last updated" date above changes and the app will tell you. Continuing to use SafeSteps after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Terms of Use

1. What SafeSteps is

SafeSteps is an educational road-safety and civic-awareness app for Kenya, produced with the Kenya Scouts Association as Project 360° Shield. It teaches road safety, runs quizzes, helps you plan a safer route, records hazards and gives you a way to raise a concern about road or traffic conduct.

SafeSteps is not an emergency service. The SOS button dials the official Kenyan emergency numbers (112 / 999) on your own phone. We do not run a monitoring centre, we do not receive your SOS, and we cannot send help. If someone is hurt, call 112 or 999 yourself.

2. Not a substitute for official guidance or training

  • The app does not replace the Traffic Act, NTSA regulations, or the instructions of a police officer or licensed driving instructor. Where the app and the law differ, the law applies.
  • First-aid content is general awareness material, not certified medical training. In a medical emergency, call for professional help.
  • Quiz results, badges and streaks are motivational. They are not a licence, a certification or proof of competence, and must not be presented as one.
  • Route and hazard information is community-reported and can be wrong, out of date or incomplete. It is a hint, not a guarantee. You remain responsible for your own safety and for following the road rules.

3. Your account

  • You must give a working email address. You are responsible for keeping your password to yourself. Passwords are 6 to 8 characters.
  • You must be old enough to consent, or have a parent, guardian, teacher or Scout leader agree on your behalf.
  • One account per person. Do not share an account or impersonate someone else.
  • You may delete your account at any time (Account → Delete My Account & Server Data).

4. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • submit false hazard reports, or reports intended to harass, defame or mislead;
  • submit a report about a named individual that you know to be untrue;
  • upload illegal content, or anyone's image without their consent;
  • attempt to break, overload, probe or gain unauthorised access to the app or its database;
  • scrape or bulk-download other people's data;
  • use the app for any commercial purpose without our written permission.

Reports you submit about road conditions may be shown back to you and counted in an anonymous community summary. Do not put anything in a report that you would not want read aloud — do not include other people's names, phone numbers or other personal details.

5. Content you submit

You keep ownership of what you write. By submitting a report you give us permission to store it, show it to you, and use it in aggregate form (counts by category and county) to build the community hazard picture. We will not publish your report with your identity attached.

Integrity reports are sent, not stored. Reports you send to a government agency through the app go to that agency and are not kept by us. Whether they act on it is up to them. Sending a report does not create a legal case, and it does not replace reporting to the police.

6. Availability

We aim to keep SafeSteps working, but it is provided "as is". The offline features — lessons, quizzes, checklists, route planner, saved data — are designed to work without a connection. Account features need one, and may be unavailable during maintenance, during an outage, or if the service is paused for exceeding a hosting plan's limits. We may change or withdraw features.
